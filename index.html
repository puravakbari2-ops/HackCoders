About JanSahay AI

What is JanSahay AI?

JanSahay AI is an AI-powered government scheme recommendation platform that helps citizens discover government schemes they are eligible for based on their personal information.

India has numerous government schemes designed to support students, farmers, women, senior citizens, unemployed individuals, small businesses, and economically weaker sections. However, many eligible citizens fail to benefit because information is scattered across different portals.

Our intelligent system simplifies this process by automatically identifying and explaining the government schemes a citizen may be eligible for, complete with benefits, required documents, and official application links.
